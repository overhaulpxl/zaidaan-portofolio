WHO IS ZAIDAAN ZALFAA?

A multidisciplinary designer and creative director focused on the intersection of typography, digital architecture, and human interaction. With a rigorous approach rooted in the Swiss International Style, I craft objective, high-utility interfaces that strip away the superfluous to reveal essential forms. My work is not meant to be friendly; it is meant to be authoritative, clear, and uncompromisingly precise.
